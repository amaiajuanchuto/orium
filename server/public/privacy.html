Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Orium is a personal mood and habit journal. This page explains what we collect, why, and how to get your data or delete your account whenever you want.

What we collect

Your account email and password are handled by our authentication provider, Supabase — we never see or store your password ourselves. Beyond that, we store only what you enter into the app: your journal entries (date, mood, energy, sleep, notes, tags) and, if you choose to fill it in, an optional profile (name, age, pronouns, work, lifestyle, hobbies, and a free-text note). None of the profile fields are required.

Why we collect it

Solely to run the app for you: storing your journal, showing your trends and streak, and surfacing patterns in your own entries. If you connect Orium to an AI assistant (via its MCP integration), that connection uses your own login and only ever reads or writes your own data — the same as using the dashboard yourself.

Who else sees it

Nobody. We don't sell your data, share it with advertisers, or run analytics trackers on it. Two infrastructure providers process it on our behalf, solely to run the service: Supabase (database and authentication) and Render (hosting). Neither uses your data for their own purposes.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. You can delete individual entries at any time from the Journal or Calendar view, or delete your entire account — see below.

Your data, your control

From Profile → Danger zone in the dashboard, you can at any time:

  • Export everything — download every entry and your profile as a JSON file.
  • Delete your account — this is immediate and permanent. Your account and every entry, tag link, and profile field tied to it are removed outright. There is no recovery window, so export first if you want to keep a copy.

For anything else — questions, corrections, or a request we haven't built self-serve tooling for yet — contact amaia.juanchuto@gmail.com.

Not a substitute for professional care

Orium is a personal journaling tool, not a medical device or a substitute for professional mental health care. If you're in crisis or need immediate support, please contact a crisis line or emergency services in your country.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continuing to use Orium after a change means you accept the updated policy.
